Homebrew Multiclass Only
Fallen God-Warriors of Blood Magic

The Darkin

Origin: ???

Once noble Shuriman Ascended who stared into the abyss and reshaped their own flesh with forbidden hemomancy, the Darkin were sealed within sentient, indestructible weapons (such as Aatrox, Rhaast, Varus, and Naafiri).

Campaign Multiclass Rule:
  • Cannot be chosen as a starting Level 1 Class.
  • Unlocked strictly via Homebrew Multiclassing when a character discovers, attunes to, and survives a battle of wills with a sealed Darkin weapon during the campaign.
  • Grants hemomantic life-drain, living-weapon transformations, and corrupting Darkin prestige abilities at the cost of ongoing Ego/Corruption saves.
Homebrew Multiclass Only
Abyssal Watchers & Icathian Rifts

The Void

Origin: ???

Manifesting from the unknowable nothingness beyond creation, The Void hungers to unmake all reality. Those who survive contact with a Void rift—like Kassadin, Kai'Sa, or Malzahar—are forever mutated by itseldritch evolution.

Campaign Multiclass Rule:
  • Cannot be chosen as a starting Level 1 Class.
  • Unlocked strictly via Homebrew Multiclassing through direct exposure to an Icathian Void Rift, Void symbiote carapace, or Whisper of the Watchers.
  • Grants adaptive Void carapace evolutions, reality-rending Force/Psychic abilities, and Voidborne mutations adjudicated with the Dungeon Master.

D&D 2024 (5.5e) Weapon Masteries Guide

Cleave If you hit a creature with a melee attack roll, you can make a second attack roll against a second creature within 5 ft of the first (damage is weapon damage die without ability mod).
Graze If your melee weapon attack misses a creature, you still deal damage to that creature equal to the ability modifier used for the attack roll (minimum of 0).
Nick When you make the extra attack granted by the Light weapon property, you can make it as part of the Attack action rather than as a Bonus Action (once per turn).
Push If you hit a creature, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from you (if the target is Large or smaller).
Sap If you hit a creature, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
Slow If you hit a creature and deal damage, you can reduce its speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
Topple If you hit a creature, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + Prof + Ability mod) or fall Prone.
Vex If you hit a creature and deal damage, you gain Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
